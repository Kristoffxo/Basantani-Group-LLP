Our Story

Allahabad made us before we made anything in it.

The Basantani name has been part of Allahabad's commercial life for three generations. What began with Madan Basantani and Asha Basantani — a household built on discipline and a good name — became, in the hands of their son Manoj Basantani, a working understanding of land, value and the long game.

Basantani Group LLP is the formal shape of that work. Manoj founded it to bring structure, compliance and scale to what the family had done informally for years. Aryan Basantani joined as co-founder and runs the LLP's day-to-day operations, bringing a modern approach to acquisition, design partnerships and how the firm presents itself to a new generation of buyers.

Prayagraj is in the middle of the largest transformation in its modern history. Our ambition is not to build the most — it is to build the parts people still want to live in when the city has finished changing.

01

Our Mission

To develop property in Prayagraj that is legally clean, structurally honest and genuinely useful to the people who occupy it — and to make the process of buying it transparent enough that a first-time buyer feels as informed as a seasoned investor.

02

Our Vision

To be the name a family in Allahabad thinks of first when they think about property — not because we are the largest, but because in a market that runs on trust, ours is the one nobody has a story about.

Values
What We Stand On

Six things we don't negotiate.

These are not aspirations. They are the conditions under which we agree to do a deal at all.

◆

Clear Title

Independent legal diligence on every parcel, every time. If the title is not clean, the deal does not happen.

◉

Honest Pricing

One price, stated plainly, with every cost disclosed up front. No moving numbers after a handshake.

◯

Build Quality

Specified materials, named contractors, and site supervision by someone whose surname is on the building.

◈

Delivery on Time

We commit to dates we have stress-tested, and we communicate the moment anything threatens one.

◇

Local Accountability

We live here. Our buyers are our neighbours. There is no version of this business where we disappear.

✦

Long Horizons

We hold what we build. Decisions get made against a twenty-year view, not a quarterly one.
