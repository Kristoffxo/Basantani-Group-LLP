About

Aryan co-founded Basantani Group LLP and represents the third generation of the Basantani family working in Allahabad. Alongside the family firm he works in the Founder's Office at Rendvo and Askken, turning founder priorities into execution across operations, growth, partnerships and go-to-market — and he is building two products of his own, Reppo and Brina.

He trained as a biomedical scientist at the University of Greenwich in the UK and is IBMS accredited. Before that he founded NoshBox, a B2B vending business, and HouseOfTourists.com in Prayagraj.
