Who We Are

A family name before it was ever a firm.

Basantani Group LLP was formed to put structure around something the family had already been doing in Allahabad for decades — buying well, building honestly, and holding for the long term.

Founded by Manoj Basantani and run alongside co-founder Aryan Basantani, the LLP brings a second generation of discipline to a city that is changing faster than it ever has. Prayagraj is expanding outward and upward at once. We intend to build the parts of it that are still standing in fifty years.

Every project we take on answers to the same question the family has always asked: would we live here ourselves?
