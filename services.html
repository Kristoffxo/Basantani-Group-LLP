Our Capabilities

From the first title search to the last handover.

Basantani Group LLP operates across the full property lifecycle in Prayagraj. We take a site from acquisition through approvals, construction and sale — and in many cases keep managing it long after.

01

Residential Development

Apartments, plotted colonies, row houses and independent homes. We design around how families in Prayagraj actually use space — cross ventilation, real storage, parking that works, and common areas that get maintained.

  • Apartment and multi-storey projects
  • Plotted residential colonies
  • Independent and row houses
  • Approvals, RERA compliance and handover
02

Commercial Development

Retail frontage, office floors, showrooms and mixed-use blocks. Our commercial work is positioned on the corridors where footfall and access are genuinely improving, not merely being marketed as such.

  • Retail and high-street frontage
  • Office and co-working floors
  • Mixed-use residential-commercial blocks
  • Warehousing and light industrial
03

Land Acquisition & Aggregation

The oldest part of the business and the part we know best. We source, verify, aggregate and hold land across Prayagraj — with legal diligence that runs ahead of the transaction, not behind it.

  • Site sourcing and feasibility
  • Title verification and legal due diligence
  • Parcel aggregation and consolidation
  • Land banking and long-hold strategy
04

Advisory & Joint Ventures

For landowners sitting on an asset they don't want to develop alone, and for investors who want exposure to Prayagraj without operating here. We structure the partnership and run the project.

  • Landowner joint-venture structuring
  • Valuation and market positioning
  • Investment and portfolio advisory
  • Development management for third parties
05

Property Management

Buildings age at the speed they are looked after. We manage completed assets — maintenance, tenancy, collections and compliance — for our own portfolio and for owners who want it handled.

  • Facility and maintenance management
  • Tenant sourcing and lease administration
  • Rent collection and reporting
  • Statutory and society compliance
06

Buyer Services

The unglamorous work that decides whether a purchase goes smoothly: paperwork, registration, home-loan coordination and the follow-through that most firms stop doing once the cheque clears.

  • Documentation and registration support
  • Home-loan and banking coordination
  • Site visits and unit selection
  • Post-possession support

Our Process

Five steps, in this order, every time.

We do not skip stages to move faster. The sequence below is what keeps projects from developing problems that only surface years later.

Start a conversation →
01

Identify & Assess

Site sourcing, location study, demand assessment and a first-pass financial model.

02

Verify

Independent title search, encumbrance check, zoning confirmation and statutory clearances.

03

Design & Approve

Architectural planning with our design partners, followed by full sanction and RERA registration.

04

Build

Named contractors, specified materials, and site supervision by a member of the firm.

05

Hand Over & Support

Registration, possession, snag resolution and ongoing management where required.
