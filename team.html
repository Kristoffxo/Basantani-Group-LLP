Founder

Manoj Basantani

मनोज बसंतानी · Founder, Basantani Group LLP

Manoj Basantani founded Basantani Group LLP to formalise work the family had already been doing in Allahabad for decades. His judgement on land — what a parcel is worth, what it will be worth, and whether it is worth the trouble — is the foundation the firm is built on.

Son of Madan Basantani and Asha Basantani, he has spent his working life in and around Prayagraj property. He leads acquisition, deal structuring and the firm's relationships with landowners across the district.

Co-Founder

Aryan Basantani

Co-Founder & Operations, Basantani Group LLP

Aryan Basantani co-founded the LLP and represents the third generation of the family working in Allahabad. Son of Manoj and Richa Basantani, he also works in the Founder's Office at Rendvo and Askken, and is building Reppo and Brina.

He brings startup operations, growth and partnerships experience, and earlier founded NoshBox and HouseOfTourists.com. He is an IBMS-accredited Biomedical Scientist from the University of Greenwich.
